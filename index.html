Platform features

Everything your club
actually needs

Stop managing leagues in WhatsApp. Stop tracking results in spreadsheets. MyClubHub replaces the chaos.

Court & alley bookings

Drag-and-drop calendar with real-time availability. Multi-book recurring sessions. Block courts for maintenance. SMS and email confirmations built in.

League management

Create divisions with custom groups. Auto-generate round-robin fixtures using the circle method. Live standings update as results come in.

Knockout competitions

Cup, Shield, Plate and custom competitions per group. Auto-seeding from league table. Finals unlock automatically when both semis are complete.

Member management

Invite players by email. Manage active, inactive and test players. Role-based access — admins see everything, players see only their own data.

Results & scoring

Enter scores from any device. Scheduled matches auto-notify players with calendar invites. League tables recalculate instantly. Full results history preserved.

Personal dashboard

Every player logs in to see their next match, league position and recent results — personalised to them, not the whole club. Mobile-first, works on any device.

How it works

Up and running
in minutes

No training needed. No manuals. If you can send a WhatsApp, you can run your club on MyClubHub.

01

Set up your club

Register your club, add your courts or alleys, and invite your members by email. Takes about 10 minutes.

02

Create your leagues

Create divisions, add players to groups, and generate a full round-robin fixture list automatically. The system handles the schedule.

03

Schedule & notify

Drag fixtures onto the booking calendar. Players are automatically notified by email and SMS with a calendar invite attached.

04

Track & champion

Enter results on your phone from the court. Standings update live. Run knockouts at the end of the season — Cup, Shield, whatever you need.
